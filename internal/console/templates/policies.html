Write a policy

A stage saves this as a rolling version: the live one stays for everyone outside it. Promote it when it has proved itself, or withdraw it.

Understood rules: {{range $i, $k := .RuleKinds}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$k}}{{end}}. Add "on_failure": "quarantine" and a device that fails this policy is quarantined on the report that fails, unless more than 5% of the fleet is failing at once, in which case nothing is quarantined and you are told. Nothing is enforced unless a policy says so. Add "remediate": true and a device that fails this policy puts it right by itself where it can: the firewall on, disk encryption started, the password length policy set, the screen lock shortened. Without it a device only reports, and nothing on it is changed. A name that is not on this list is refused rather than ignored, so a typo cannot become a policy that requires nothing.
