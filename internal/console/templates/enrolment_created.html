Enrolment link ready

Give this to whoever has the device. It is shown once. We keep only a hash of it, so it cannot be read back, and if it is lost the answer is to make another one.

The link

This is for a laptop or desktop running your organisation’s device agent, and it points at this console’s own address. Android phones are invited from the Android page instead, with a link from Google.

The device sends this link’s token together with a certificate signing request. What comes back is its identity certificate, signed by your organisation’s own authority, which is what it uses to prove who it is from then on.
