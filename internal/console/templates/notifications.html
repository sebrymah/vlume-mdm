Point a topic somewhere

A chat address is kept encrypted and never shown again, because anyone holding one can post into that channel; an address inside this console’s own network is refused. An email address is shown in full, because noticing that notifications have been going to somebody who left is the thing most worth noticing on this page. Several addresses can be separated by commas.

Where your mail comes from

By default notifications are sent from this console’s own address. Add your own domain and they come from you instead, signed, so they reach inboxes rather than spam folders.
